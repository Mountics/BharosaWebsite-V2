About Us

Our name means trust, and our values spell it out

Bharosa Community Services is a registered NDIS provider delivering trusted, person-centred support services across Australia. Established in Brisbane in 2021, we support participants of all ages across Queensland and Tasmania, with plans to keep expanding nationally.

We offer everyday supports, Supported Independent Living, Support Coordination, and specialist high-intensity and behaviour support services, all shaped around what each person needs to live confidently and independently.

Our Vision

Inclusive NDIS Support Services Across Australia

We envision a society where people with disability are empowered to live freely and fully, without compromise. Our vision is to foster inclusion, confidence and quality of life through long-term relationships and trusted support shaped by respect, consistency and a genuine commitment to wellbeing.

Before any service begins, we take time to understand what each participant needs so they get the most benefit from our support, and we encourage community participation through meaningful occupational and creative activities that build confidence and quality of life.

We uplift lives through high-quality, person-centred NDIS support that meets official guidelines and each participant's individual needs.

What is an NDIS service provider?

An NDIS service provider is an organisation approved to deliver supports to people funded under the National Disability Insurance Scheme (NDIS). At Bharosa, we provide trusted and culturally respectful NDIS Support Services designed to help participants live independently and confidently.

What does Bharosa do as an NDIS service provider?

As an NDIS service provider, Bharosa supports participants by delivering services aligned with their individual goals. Our role is to provide reliable, person-centred support that promotes independence, wellbeing, and community participation.

What NDIS Support Services does Bharosa provide?

Our NDIS Support Services may include:

  • Assistance with daily living activities
  • Personal care and in-home support
  • Community access and social participation
  • Life skills and capacity building
  • Support coordination
  • Short-term accommodation and respite support

Services are tailored to each participant’s NDIS plan and personal needs.

Who is eligible for NDIS support?

NDIS support is available to people who:

  • Are under 65 years of age
  • Are Australian citizens, permanent residents, or eligible visa holders
  • Have a permanent disability that significantly affects daily life

Supports must be considered reasonable and necessary under NDIS guidelines.

What activities can NDIS funding be used for?

NDIS funding can be used for activities that help participants:

  • Maintain independence at home
  • Build everyday and social skills
  • Participate in community and cultural activities
  • Improve wellbeing, safety, and quality of life

All activities must align with the participant’s NDIS goals.

What does NDIS funding not cover?

NDIS does not fund:

  • Everyday living expenses such as rent or groceries
  • Medical treatments covered by Medicare
  • Supports unrelated to a person’s disability
  • Unsafe or unnecessary services

Funding is only provided for disability-related supports that meet NDIS criteria.
