About the home

40 The Avenue is a single-storey house with 4 bedrooms, 2 bathrooms, and 2 parking spaces in Heathwood, QLD. The property has a land area of 500 m². It is within Brisbane local government, with bushfire and flood overlays detected.

Support model

  • 24/7 onsite support staff
  • Support ratios available: 1:1 / 1:2 / 1:3 (based on needs and compatibility)
  • Active overnight / sleepover support (based on participant needs)
  • Tailored support aligned with individual NDIS goals
  • Experienced and culturally responsive support team

Location highlights

  • Nearby parks and green spaces: Easy access to Heathwood Park, Spotted Gum Street Park and Sergeant Dan Stiller Memorial Reserve for outdoor recreation and relaxation.
  • Shopping centres and supermarkets: Convenient access to Forest Lake Shopping Centre, Richlands Plaza, Woolworths Doolandella and local retail amenities.
  • Public transport: Local bus services connecting Heathwood to surrounding suburbs and Brisbane CBD, with Richlands Train Station a short drive away.
  • Medical centres and allied health: Access to Forest Lake Medical & Dental Centre, Richlands Medical Centre and nearby healthcare and allied health services.

Suitable for people who

  • Are looking for a stable and supportive shared living environment
  • Value independence with structured support
  • Are compatible with shared living arrangements
  • Have SIL funding in their NDIS plan
