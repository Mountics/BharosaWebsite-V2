Our Supported Independent Living services are built around what you actually need, not a one-size-fits-all care package. When you move into a Bharosa SIL home, your support plan is tailored to your NDIS goals, your daily routine, and your preferences.

Personal Care and Daily Living

24/7 Support and Supervision

Our support workers are available around the clock. Whether you need overnight active support or sleepover staff, we roster the right team so you always have someone you trust nearby.

Support with planning, shopping, and cooking meals that suit your dietary needs and preferences. We also help with budgeting and grocery management.

Help with cleaning, laundry, garden maintenance, and keeping your home safe and comfortable, so you can focus on the things that matter to you.

Trained staff to assist with medication reminders, administration, and coordination with your health team or GP.

We encourage and support you to stay connected: attending events, visiting family, joining community activities, and building friendships in Brisbane and the local area.

Skill Building and Independence

Our support workers actively help you build the skills to live more independently over time, from cooking to budgeting to public transport navigation.

Is It Right for You?

Who is eligible for SIL funding?

SIL funding is available to NDIS participants who require significant, ongoing support to live safely and work toward greater independence at home. Eligibility is assessed by the NDIS based on your individual needs, goals, and evidence from allied health professionals such as your Occupational Therapist.

The NDIS approves SIL funding based on a Roster of Care (ROC) submitted by your provider, which outlines the support hours, staffing ratios, and care requirements for your living situation.

If you're unsure whether you qualify, our team can help you understand your options and work with your support coordinator to build the case for SIL funding in your plan review.

You are generally eligible for SIL if you:

  • Need regular daily support at home that cannot reasonably be provided informally
  • Have disability-related support needs that are consistent with the NDIS reasonable and necessary criteria
  • Are under 65 years of age and an Australian citizen, permanent resident, or holder of a protected special category visa
  • Live in Australia

FAQs

Common questions about SIL housing and support.

What is SIL housing?

SIL housing refers to shared or individual accommodation where an NDIS participant receives Supported Independent Living support services. The NDIS funds the daily support (personal care, household assistance, 24/7 staffing) while the participant typically covers rent through the Disability Support Pension and Rent Assistance.

How much does SIL housing cost?

The cost depends on your support needs, the number of housemates, and whether active overnight or sleepover support is required. For eligible NDIS participants, SIL support costs are funded through your NDIS plan under Core Supports: Assistance with Daily Life. Your NDIS planner or support coordinator can help clarify what’s funded in your specific plan.

What is the difference between SIL and SDA?

SDA (Specialist Disability Accommodation) refers to the physical property, the bricks and mortar, designed for people with high support needs. SIL refers to the support services delivered inside that home. Some participants have both SDA and SIL funding; others have SIL only and live in standard rental properties or Bharosa-owned homes.

Who pays for SIL accommodation in Brisbane?

The NDIS pays for the support services (staff, care, supervision). The participant typically pays rent from their Disability Support Pension, with Rent Assistance often available through Centrelink. Bharosa can walk you through exactly what to expect financially before you make any decisions.

Can I choose where I live with SIL?

Yes. You have the right to choose your home and living arrangement. If our Morayfield vacancy isn’t the right fit, we can support you in an SDA property of your choice in Brisbane, or in your own home if you have SIL funding in your plan.

Does Bharosa have current SIL vacancies?

Yes. We currently have SIL housing vacancies in Queensland, and we welcome SDA, MTA, and respite enquiries across Queensland and Tasmania. Contact our team to discuss eligibility, view a property, and understand the move-in process.

How do I apply for SIL funding through the NDIS?

SIL funding is typically recommended through allied health assessments, particularly from your Occupational Therapist, supported by a Roster of Care. Your support coordinator plays a key role in preparing the evidence needed for your NDIS plan review. Bharosa can work with your coordinator to help build this case.

Is Bharosa a registered NDIS provider for SIL?

Yes. Bharosa Community Services is a fully registered NDIS provider in Queensland and Tasmania, registered under the dedicated NDIS SIL registration group and meeting all SIL Practice Standards under the NDIS Quality and Safeguards Commission.

Is SIL now a separate NDIS registration group?

Yes. From 1 July 2026, Supported Independent Living is a mandatory registration requirement and sits in its own NDIS registration group (Registration Group 0138), separate from the older shared-living support category. Providers must be registered specifically for SIL and meet the new SIL module of the NDIS Practice Standards. Bharosa is registered under this new group.

Does Bharosa meet the new mandatory SIL registration requirements?

Yes. Bharosa Community Services is registered under the new NDIS SIL registration group and complies with the SIL-specific Practice Standards introduced on 1 July 2026. This means our Supported Independent Living services are delivered in full compliance with the latest NDIS quality and safeguarding rules.
