High Intensity Daily Personal Activities (HIDPA)

Our High Intensity Daily Personal Activities (HIDPA) Support service is designed to provide safe, high-quality, and person-centered care for individuals with complex health needs. We support individuals in their homes and community settings, ensuring they receive the necessary health-related assistance to live safely and independently.

Specialist Care

What Support and Care We Provide

We provide expert care for individuals requiring specialized health supports that comply with industry regulations and are assessed and monitored by registered nurses, including:

Complex Bowel Care

Assistance with bowel management, including medication administration, digital stimulation, and enema support to maintain comfort and health.

Enteral Feeding & Management

Specialized support for individuals requiring feeding through a nasogastric or gastrostomy tube, ensuring proper nutrition and hydration.

Severe Dysphagia Management

Safe mealtime assistance, including texture-modified diets and swallowing techniques to prevent choking and aspiration.

Tracheostomy Care

Skilled care for individuals with tracheostomies, including cleaning, suctioning, and monitoring for complications to ensure safe breathing.

Urinary Catheter Management

Expert assistance in maintaining and changing urinary catheters, reducing the risk of infections and ensuring comfort.

Ventilation Support

Professional support for individuals using ventilators or other respiratory aids, ensuring equipment function and emergency response readiness.

Subcutaneous Injections

Safe and timely administration of prescribed injectable medications, such as insulin or anticoagulants, to manage health conditions.

Complex Wound Care

Professional management of chronic or post-surgical wounds, including dressing changes, infection prevention, and ongoing monitoring.

Stoma Care

Assistance with colostomy or ileostomy care, including cleaning, appliance changes, and ensuring overall stoma health and hygiene.

Pressure Care and Wound Management

Preventative care and repositioning support to reduce the risk of pressure ulcers and skin breakdown for individuals with limited mobility.

Seizure Management (including Midazolam administration)

Supervision and emergency response for individuals at high risk of seizures, including administering emergency medications like Midazolam.

Mealtime Preparation and Delivery

Support in preparing and serving nutritious meals based on dietary needs, including modified textures and feeding assistance when required.

Is It Right for You?

Who this service is for

At Bharosa, we are committed to delivering compassionate, high-quality care that empowers individuals to live safely, comfortably, and with dignity.

Our HIDPA supports are available to:

  • Children and young people in foster care, residential care, and aftercare services.
  • Adults receiving Disability and Mental Health Services.
  • Any NDIS participants requiring high-density personal care.
